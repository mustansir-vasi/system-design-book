# Learn System Design Step by Step

A beginner book on system design, written by Mustansir Vasi. It teaches one idea at a time, uses plain words and defines every term before it is used.

## The three parts

| Part | Title | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| Part 1 | Foundations | 1 to 6 |
| Part 2 | Data and Building Blocks | 7 to 15 |
| Part 3 | Designing Real Systems and Interviews | 16 to 26 |

Parts 1 and 2 make up Volume 1. Part 3 is Volume 2.

## Chapters

| Chapter | PDF |
|---|---|
| 1. What is system design | [chapter-1-what-is-system-design.pdf](pdf/chapter-1-what-is-system-design.pdf) |

Chapter 1 is finished (12 pages). It covers what a system and a design are, requirements, trade-offs and the problem and block loop. It ends with a one minute summary, practice problems, a revision sheet and further reading. Chapters 2 to 6 are next, one at a time.
